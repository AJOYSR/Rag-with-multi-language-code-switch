# Literature Matrix

**Verification levels**
- `metadata-verified`: title, authors and venue confirmed on an official page (proceedings, ACL Anthology, arXiv abs page) in this project.
- `abstract-read`: abstract or official summary read. Findings below come from that summary only.
- `full-read`: paper read in full; notes in `notes/<bibkey>.md`. **Only this level may support specific claims in the paper.**
- `found-only`: surfaced by search; existence not yet confirmed on an official page.

## D. Gap status (updated 2026-10-23 after M1 full reads)

- **Already known [FACT, full-read]:** abstention degrades in low-resource languages incl. Bengali (A2, A7); romanized input degrades reliability and shifts models toward "insufficient information" (A4) or weaker refusal (A9); multilingual RAG models trade hallucination against over-abstention, Bengali included (A3); sufficiency signals help selective generation in English (A1); English-tuned thresholds lose effectiveness on other languages (A7, pooled, F1-selected) and flip decisions on paired code-mixed input (A8, classification).
- **Apparently unresolved:** target-risk transfer of an English-selected abstention threshold to BN / RBN / CM renderings of the same RAG item under controlled evidence sufficiency; direct comprehension-vs-calibration decomposition. See `G1_novelty_memo.md`.
- **Still open checks:** "Probe Before you Answer" (Scholar-only listing, unresolved); ACL Anthology queries and Google Scholar "cited by" for A1/A2 to be repeated manually (search agent could only partly cover them).
